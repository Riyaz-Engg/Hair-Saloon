ABOUT A TOUCH OF CLASS

Modern. Premium. Stylish.

Located in the heart of Bhopal near Roshanpura Square, A Touch Of Class is a premium barber shop and unisex hair & beauty salon dedicated to expert styling and personal grooming.

We believe that personal style starts with understanding what works best for you. Our experienced team provides an unmatched level of attention to detail, whether you're here for a precision haircut, a rejuvenating hair spa, or a comprehensive beauty treatment for a special occasion.

Step into a space designed for relaxation and leave looking and feeling your absolute best.
